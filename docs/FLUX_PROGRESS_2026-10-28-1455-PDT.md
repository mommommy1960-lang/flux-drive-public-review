## Finite particle lifetime sensitivity: a bounded mathematical check

A previously reported conditional Kerr test-geodesic example assumed that a short-lived neutral pion decayed immediately at its creation point. This update adds a numerical sensitivity study using the Particle Data Group's measured pion lifetime and a finite set of later decay times. For the stated illustrative stellar-mass scale and a selected decay direction, the qualitative capture/escape classification did not change in the sampled cases.

**Scientific boundary:** This is a computational test of one assumed kinematic configuration, not a measurement, a continuous interval-certified proof for all decay times or angles, a validated particle-production rate, or evidence of useful energy extraction. The modeled black hole still gains net energy. There is no demonstrated propulsion or FTL result.

**Other research gates:** The proposed rotating-mass measurement bench lacks real calibration, source/sham controls, noise spectra and sensitivity validation, so CAL-00 remains NO-GO. Four patent categories remain preliminary documentary leads; no official case-specific family-wide expiration/abandonment or freedom-to-operate status was authenticated.
