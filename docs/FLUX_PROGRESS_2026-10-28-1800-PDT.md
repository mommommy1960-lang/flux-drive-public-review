# Flux Drive public progress | 2026-10-28 18:00 PDT

**Research-only, no propulsion demonstration.** A new conditional mathematical angular-screening calculation was completed for a neutral-pion decay near a rotating Kerr black hole. It distinguishes the mere possibility of a negative conserved photon energy from the stricter requirement that the two photons initially move in the intended radial directions. Independent numerical sampling checked the calculation. The result is not a verified full capture/escape probability, not a physical reaction-production rate, and not an energy-positive engine. Earlier energy-ledger checks still reject useful net extraction in the selected example.

The laboratory rotating-mass measurement concept remains blocked by unmeasured systematic errors and missing calibration data. The four patent-screening categories have no authenticated case-specific legal status or family-wide freedom-to-operate determination. No experiments, purchases, external commitments, patent filings, FTL claims or protected-branch merges.
