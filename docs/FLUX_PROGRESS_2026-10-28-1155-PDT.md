# Flux Drive research progress — October 28, 2026 (11:55 PDT)

**Status:** Mathematical and documentary research only. No physical experiment, propulsion engine, faster-than-light travel, patent clearance or hardware build.

An independent calculation clarified an important distinction in a restricted rotating-black-hole particle model: a black hole can gain total angular momentum while its *dimensionless* spin parameter decreases because its mass also grows. This does not demonstrate net energy extraction. The result remains conditional on unverified particle-production physics and particle trajectories; it is not a general prohibition on other energy-extraction processes.

A separate inverse sensitivity calculation found that simply enlarging a hypothetical rotating laboratory mass to reach an assumed detection threshold produces an implausibly large source. This is a modeling rejection, not a measured detector result. The CAL-00 calibration gate remains closed pending traceable instrument noise, reversal controls and independent artifact rejection.

Patent research retained the four categories of gravitation, beam instrumentation, precision gravimetry and rotor/bearing systems. No authenticated case-specific USPTO legal histories or family-wide rights were obtained, so no expired/abandoned-patent clearance or freedom-to-operate claim is made.
