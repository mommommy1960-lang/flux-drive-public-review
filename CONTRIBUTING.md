# Synthetic challenge participation

This is an educational, fictional data challenge for adults. It is free, voluntary, and has no prize, cash value, drawing, employment offer, or promise of research credit beyond optional public acknowledgment. The contribution board is a review-status list, not a scientific ranking or guarantee of publication.

## How to take part
1. Run the small standard-library Python example in `sandbox/` or inspect the CSV manually.
2. Open a `[Challenge]` issue with a fictional dataset or a specific critique and a reproducible command.
3. Keep your issue to synthetic data and reasoning. Do not post real instrument data, unpublished inventions, build recipes, credentials, medical or financial details, phone numbers, addresses, or anyone else's information. Do not upload executable attachments or links you expect others to run. Maintainers will not run arbitrary submitted code.
4. Say `yes` or `no` in **Public credit**. `yes` allows your GitHub username and issue link to appear on the board *after a maintainer reviews the issue*. You may change to `no` later; this removes future board display, but public issue history and platform archives may persist. If in doubt, use `no`.
5. Maintainers may ask questions, reject unsafe or off-topic content, hide/delete content where platform tools allow, and close or lock threads. We do not promise to evaluate every entry or integrate any idea.

You own your original submission. By posting it publicly you permit us to display, discuss, reproduce, and adapt the *synthetic dataset and critique* for review of this challenge with attribution to your GitHub username if you opt in to public credit. No assignment of patents, inventions, or private engineering information is requested. Do not submit material you cannot grant these limited permissions for. A separate written agreement would be needed for any private design collaboration or broader use. If you want a submission withdrawn from organizer use, open an issue or contact the maintainer through GitHub; GitHub copies/history may remain.

## Review and integration
A moderator first screens for safety, privacy, third-party rights, spam and relevance, then may apply `reviewed-entry`. A reproducible critique gets `validated-counterexample` only after a maintainer reproduces it from the submitted fictional data. The private research system never automatically ingests a public issue or executes contributed code. A qualified human reviews any possible lesson before separate implementation and tests. No fictional result supports a claim of physical propulsion.
