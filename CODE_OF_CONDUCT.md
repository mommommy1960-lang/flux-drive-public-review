# Community conduct

Discuss the evidence and methods, not the person. Criticism and null results are welcome. Harassment, threats, hate, impersonation, spam, doxxing, attempts to expose confidential material, unsafe hardware directions, and deliberate false claims of verified propulsion are not welcome.

Maintainers may edit, hide, remove, close, or lock contributions under GitHub's moderation tools. Report harmful public material using GitHub's report tools. For private security or accidental disclosure, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not repeat the material in another public issue. This community is intended for adults; do not submit information about children.

There is no prize and no obligation to publish or accept a contribution. Public acknowledgment is opt-in and can be removed from the organizer's current board; platform history may remain.
